Design Basis
============

Agreed design basis and authored project defaults, distilled in
``planning/design_basis.md``:

- Fresh-to-moist, freely drained woodland (not a wetland).
- 300 mm upper organic Layer A over 600 mm mineral Layer B.
- Generally 75 mm settled wood-chip mulch.
- Upper layer organic matter 5–8% dry mass; lower layer 1–3%.
- pH 5.0–6.5; saturated-paste EC ≤ 2 dS/m; SAR ≤ 4.
- Mineral texture: sand 50–70%, silt 15–35%, clay 10–20% of the fine mineral
  fraction; total silt plus clay 30–50% on that same basis.
- 30 m³ net usable soil allocation per large tree.

These depths, limits, and frequencies are authored project defaults, not
official CWH-wide limits, and require site-specific confirmation before
tender issue.

Unresolved pre-tender items include the project schedule (site and authority,
plant palette and subzone, footprint and quantities, per-tree allocations),
geotechnical/utility/excavation constraints, groundwater, designed drainage
and authorized outlet, and environmental QP contaminant criteria with a
sampling plan. Supplied phosphorus (324 ppm) and potassium (1,956 ppm)
figures are retained as unverified references, not enforceable limits.

The City of Vancouver GRI planting guidelines PDF is excluded as a source by
explicit project preference.
