## Project intent

A detailed tender specification for new trees and woodland understory in a
constructed urban bed over compacted fill or disturbed construction soil,
within the Coastal Western Hemlock (CWH) biogeoclimatic zone.

## Agreed design basis and draft defaults

- Fresh-to-moist, freely drained woodland (not a wetland).
- 300 mm upper organic Layer A over 600 mm mineral Layer B.
- Generally 75 mm settled wood-chip mulch.
- Upper layer organic matter 5–8% dry mass; lower layer 1–3%.
- pH 5.0–6.5; saturated-paste EC ≤ 2 dS/m; SAR ≤ 4.
- Mineral texture: sand 50–70%, silt 15–35%, clay 10–20%, summing to 100% of
  the fine mineral fraction; total silt plus clay 30–50% on that same basis.
- Table 1A distinguishes total-medium dry-mass percentages using the measured
  fine mineral fraction F.
- 30 m³ net usable soil allocation per large tree.
- Soil and mulch depths, the 30 m³ allocation, and physical limits and
  frequencies are authored project defaults, not official CWH-wide limits.

The specification also includes laboratory protocols, independent lot
sampling, subgrade preparation, drainage, a full-depth trial bed, five hold
points, field verification, corrections, two-year maintenance, and
measurement/payment rules. Subgrade and drainage must be accepted before
imported soil covers them.

## Unresolved items (pre-tender)

Before tender issue, fill the project schedule:

- Site and authority having jurisdiction.
- Plant palette and CWH subzone.
- Bed footprint and soil quantities; per-tree soil allocations.
- Geotechnical, utility, and excavation constraints.
- Groundwater conditions.
- Designed drainage and authorized outlet.
- Environmental QP contaminant criteria and sampling plan.
- Confirm numerical defaults with the project team and available
  laboratory/supplier capability.

The draft is not issued-for-construction and not professionally certified.

## Supplied nutrient figures (unverified)

Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium are report-and-review properties without
fixed universal woodland targets. The user-supplied figures P = 324 ppm and
K = 1,956 ppm are preserved in the specification as unverified references,
not enforceable limits or fertilization instructions. Source, extraction
method, total-versus-extractable basis, and suitability remain unresolved.

## Source preferences and exclusions

- Do not use the City of Vancouver GRI planting guidelines PDF
  (`vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-gri-planting-guidelines.pdf`); it was
  excluded by explicit user preference from an earlier plant project.
- The specification relies on BC ecology descriptions (CWH zone and western
  hemlock), NRCS physical-soil guidance, CCME compost guidance, BC OMRR and
  soil relocation guidance, and a nutrient-method reference. Applicable
  municipal requirements must be coordinated before tender issue.
- Full source list: Appendix A of the current specification.
